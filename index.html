Compliance Library

A reference guide to Fair Housing Act compliance, prohibited terms, and safe alternatives.

👨‍👩‍👧

Familial Status

Avoid language that implies preference for or against families with children. One of the most commonly violated protected classes.

Prohibited Terms:
perfect for couplesno childrenadult communityquiet households
Safe Alternatives:
spacious living areastranquil settingopen floor plan
🌍

Race & National Origin

Describing a neighborhood's demographic composition or cultural character can constitute illegal steering under the Fair Housing Act.

Prohibited Terms:
diverse neighborhoodintegrated areapredominantly [group]
Safe Alternatives:
vibrant communityclose-knit neighborhoodthriving area
♿

Disability

Avoid language that implies limitations based on physical or mental ability. Focus on property features, not who can use them.

Prohibited Terms:
able-bodiedperfect for healthywalk to everything
Safe Alternatives:
accessible layoutnear amenitiesconvenient location
⛪

Religion

References to religious institutions or holiday traditions can create an implied preference or limitation on protected grounds.

Prohibited Terms:
walk to churchChristian communitynear synagogue
Safe Alternatives:
near community centerswalkable area
👶

Age / Sex

Targeting language at specific genders or age groups (other than permitted senior housing) violates the Fair Housing Act.

Prohibited Terms:
perfect for bacheloryoung womenretiree paradise
Safe Alternatives:
flexible layoutlow-maintenance living
🏡

Steering Risk Phrases

Implied steering guides buyers toward or away from areas based on protected characteristics, even without explicit mention.

High-Risk Patterns:
up-and-coming areachanging neighborhoodtransitional community
Safe Alternatives:
growing marketstrong investment area
